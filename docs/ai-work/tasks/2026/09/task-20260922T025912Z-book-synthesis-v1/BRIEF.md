# HIGH authorship brief — book synthesis

Write the v1 Typst book from reviewed evidence. Major families are normalized facts,
rolldown/duplication, rollups/materialized aggregates, embedded documents, append-only
history/snapshots, and external read copies/caches. Variants, technology, topology, and
workload remain separate axes.

Each entry uses: quick choice; thrives/perishes; scenarios; mechanism; read/write/storage/
correctness/operations costs; direct evidence; boundaries; reproduction. Reuse concept
chapters instead of repeating mechanisms. Explain indexes versus hot-row serialization,
optimistic versus pessimistic control, derived-state maintenance, history/answerability,
expiry and clock authority, cache fencing, placement, and honest benchmark reading.

Use supermarkets, e-commerce, auctions, top-10 dashboards, ticketing, reserved seating,
configuration portals, and donor portals where useful, but label every transfer as direct
evidence or analogy. Do not present JSONB as a native document-store comparison or Redis
as an authoritative key-value comparison. Do not turn laptop topologies into production
capacity guidance.

Produce a draft PDF and submit it with the Decision Log for independent HIGH review.
